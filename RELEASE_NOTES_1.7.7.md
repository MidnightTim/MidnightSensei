# MidnightSensei 1.7.7 Release Notes

A small follow-up release fixing a scoring bug reported after 1.7.6 shipped.

## Death Knight

**Unholy — Death Strike.** No longer treated as an expected part of the core rotation. It's a survival/self-heal spender you can reach for situationally, not something you're expected to weave into your damage rotation on cooldown — so skipping it no longer counts against your score.
